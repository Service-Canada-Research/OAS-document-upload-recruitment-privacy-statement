Privacy Notice – User Research and Usability Testing

Please take a moment to read this privacy notice. By proceeding with your participation in this research session, you are agreeing to provide personal information to Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC).

Why your information is collected

Personal information collected through this research activity is collected under the authority of section 39 of the Department of Employment and Social Development Act and in accordance with the Privacy Act for the purpose of conducting usability testing and user research to improve digital services within ESDC.

Your participation is voluntary. If you do not wish to provide your personal information, you will not be able to participate in this study.

What personal information is collected

  • Contact information (such as name and email address).
  • Compensation-related information (such as mailing address).
  • Session recordings captured through Microsoft Teams.
  • Observations made by moderators during research sessions.
  • Information collected through recruitment tools such as surveys.

How your information is used

Session recordings are retained to allow the research team to review interactions, identify usability issues, verify observations, and maintain accurate research notes.

Your contact and compensation information is used solely for scheduling sessions and providing participation incentives.

Participation without being recorded

  • Sessions are normally recorded through Microsoft Teams.
  • If you do not wish to be recorded, the session cannot continue.

Confidentiality and Access to Information

All information collected remains confidential and is used only to improve ESDC digital services.
