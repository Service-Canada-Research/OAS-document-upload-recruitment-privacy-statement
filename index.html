Privacy Notice – User Research and Usability Testing

Please take a moment to read this privacy notice. By proceeding with your participation in this research session, you are agreeing to provide personal information to Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC).

Why your information is collected

Personal information collected through this research activity is collected under the authority of section 39 of the Department of Employment and Social Development Act and in accordance with the Privacy Act for the purpose of conducting usability testing and user research to improve digital services within ESDC. This includes observing how participants interact with live web environments (secure and non-secure) during moderated sessions, recording sessions for review and analysis, and contacting participants to schedule sessions and provide compensation for their participation.

Your participation is voluntary. If you do not wish to provide your personal information, you will not be able to participate in this study.

What personal information is collected

The following personal information may be collected in connection with your participation:

  • Contact information (such as name and email address) used to schedule your session and provide your compensation.
  • Compensation-related information (such as mailing address) required to deliver your compensation for your participation.
  • Session recordings, including audio and video captured via Microsoft Teams, for the purpose of post-session review and analysis.
  • Observations made by moderators during the session. In some sessions, you will be asked to perform tasks within a live environment, and moderators may incidentally observe personal information displayed on your screen, which may include sensitive personal information (e.g., personal identifiers or account-related information).
  • Information collected through recruitment tools, including online pop-up surveys (e.g., Adobe Analytics), which may be used to identify potential participants.

How your information is used

Session recordings are retained to allow the research team to review interactions, identify usability issues, map areas where participants encounter difficulty, verify observations, and maintain accurate research notes. Recordings are stored securely in SharePoint and are accessible only to authorized members of the project team. Moderators hold the required security clearance and are kept current on applicable privacy standards. Personal information observed incidentally during live sessions is not recorded separately, extracted, or used for any purpose other than the immediate context of the session. Once this information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted.

Your contact and compensation information is used solely for the purpose of scheduling your session and delivering your participation incentive and is not shared or used for any other purpose.

Session invitations and scheduling communications are sent using GC Forms and Microsoft Teams. These platforms are used in accordance with Government of Canada policies and security requirements.

The results of this research may also be used for policy analysis, research, and evaluation purposes within ESDC. These uses of your personal information will never result in a decision being made about you.

Personal information collected through recruitment tools is used solely to identify, contact, and schedule participants for research activities.

Participation without being recorded

  • Sessions are normally recorded via Microsoft Teams to support post-session analysis. If you do not wish to be recorded, please let the moderator know before the session begins.
  • If you do not wish to be recorded, we will not be able to continue with this session.

Your consent to participate and to be recorded will be obtained prior to the session, either in writing or verbally, and will be confirmed at the start of the recording.

Confidentiality and access to information

All information collected remains confidential and is used only to improve ESDC's digital services. Access to session recordings and research data is limited to members of the project team. Your identity will not be shared with anyone outside of the project team, and your personal information will not appear in any reports. Any video clips used for internal presentations will be de-identified, including through techniques such as blurring or removing personal information from the recordings.

Your participation is confidential and will not affect any current or future interactions with ESDC or Service Canada.

When your information is disclosed

The results of this research activity may be used and/or disclosed within ESDC/Service Canada or with other Government of Canada institutions for policy analysis, research, and/or evaluation purposes. These uses or disclosures will never result in the sharing of your identity or in an administrative decision being made about you.

Retention and disposal

Personal information collected through this activity will be retained for the period required to fulfill the purposes described above, in accordance with ESDC's retention and disposal schedules and the Library and Archives of Canada Act . Once retention requirements are met, personal information will be securely disposed of in accordance with applicable Government of Canada procedures.

Your rights under the Privacy Act

Your personal information is protected under the Privacy Act and administered in accordance with the Department of Employment and Social Development Act and other applicable laws.

You have the right to request access to your personal information and to request corrections if it is inaccurate or incomplete. To make a request, use the Access to Information and Privacy Online Request service.

Refer to the Personal Information Bank Outreach Activities – PSU 938 in Info Source for a description of how personal information related to this activity is managed.

If you are not satisfied with how your personal information is handled, you have the right to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada .
