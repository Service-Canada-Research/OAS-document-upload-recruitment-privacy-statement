<h1>Privacy Notice – User Research and Usability Testing</h1>

<div class="notice">
<p>
Please take a moment to read this privacy notice. By proceeding with your participation in this research session, you are agreeing to provide personal information to Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC).
</p>
</div>

<h2>Why your information is collected</h2>

<p>
Personal information collected through this research activity is collected under the authority of section 39 of the
<a href="https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/H-5.7/page-1.html" target="_blank">
Department of Employment and Social Development Act
</a>
and in accordance with the Privacy Act for the purpose of conducting usability testing and user research to improve digital services within ESDC.
</p>

<p>
Your participation is voluntary. If you do not wish to provide your personal information, you will not be able to participate in this study.
</p>

<h2>What personal information is collected</h2>

<ul>
<li>Contact information (such as name and email address) used to schedule your session and provide your compensation.</li>
<li>Compensation-related information (such as mailing address) required to deliver your compensation for your participation.</li>
<li>Session recordings, including audio and video captured via Microsoft Teams, for the purpose of post-session review and analysis.</li>
<li>Observations made by moderators during the session.</li>
<li>Information collected through recruitment tools, including online pop-up surveys (e.g., Adobe Analytics), which may be used to identify potential participants.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How your information is used</h2>

<p>
Session recordings are retained to allow the research team to review interactions, identify usability issues, map areas where participants encounter difficulty, verify observations, and maintain accurate research notes.
</p>

<p>
Your contact and compensation information is used solely for the purpose of scheduling your session and delivering your participation incentive and is not shared or used for any other purpose.
</p>

<p>
Session invitations and scheduling communications are sent using GC Forms and Microsoft Teams.
</p>

<h2>Participation without being recorded</h2>

<ul>
<li>Sessions are normally recorded via Microsoft Teams to support post-session analysis.</li>
<li>If you do not wish to be recorded, we will not be able to continue with this session.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Confidentiality and access to information</h2>

<p>
All information collected remains confidential and is used only to improve ESDC's digital services.
</p>

<h2>When your information is disclosed</h2>

<p>
Research results may be used within ESDC/Service Canada or with other Government of Canada institutions for policy analysis, research, and evaluation purposes.
</p>

<h2>Retention and disposal</h2>

<p>
Personal information collected through this activity will be retained in accordance with ESDC retention schedules and the
<a href="https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-7.7/" target="_blank">
Library and Archives of Canada Act
</a>.
</p>

<h2>Your rights under the Privacy Act</h2>

<p>
Your personal information is protected under the
<a href="https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-21/page-1.html" target="_blank">
Privacy Act
</a>.
</p>

To request access to your personal information or request corrections, use the
<a href="https://atip-aiprp.apps.gc.ca/atip/welcome.do" target="_blank">
Access to Information and Privacy Online Request
</a>
service.
</p>
